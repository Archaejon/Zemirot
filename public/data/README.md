# Song data — not covered by the MIT licence

The files in this folder are a snapshot of two third-party sources:

- the [Zemirot Database](https://www.zemirotdatabase.org/) index and song records
  (`library.json`, `meta.json`, `songs/`), and
- a Syrian (Aleppo) Baqashot collection transcribed from a printed songbook
  (`baqashot/`).

They are reproduced for reading and study. They are **not** part of the MIT
licence that covers the Zemirot Studio source code, and no rights to them are
granted by this repository. Song texts, translations, notes, and anything else
here remain with their contributors; check the
[source copyright policy](https://www.zemirotdatabase.org/copyright.php) before
redistributing them.

Recordings and video are never bundled. The app links to the public URLs on the
source site and streams from there.
