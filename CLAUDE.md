# CLAUDE.md

## Session journal (required)

- **At the start of every session**, read `JOURNAL.md` before doing anything else, and use it as the record of past work, open issues and next steps.
- **At the end of every session**, add a new dated entry at the top of `JOURNAL.md` (below the header) covering what was done, the current state, open issues and next steps.
- A task is not complete until both steps are done.
